# Public content policy

The website is an early public product direction. It is not evidence of an
available application, supported release, supported document format, customer
entitlement, security certification, or service-level commitment.

## Allowed content

- The document-review problem and intended audience.
- High-level principles: preserve source context, make scope visible, connect
  notes to evidence, expose gaps, and keep judgment human.
- A clearly labeled conceptual illustration that is not presented as product
  UI or a screenshot.
- Website operation, contribution, security, licensing, and deployment facts
  proven by this repository.
- Research-list behavior proven by the website source and privacy notice.

## Content requiring separate acceptance

- Product availability, pricing, platform, or release statements.
- Format, model, integration, collaboration, deployment, or performance
  capabilities.
- Security, privacy, compliance, certification, retention, residency, or
  training-use claims beyond the public website itself.
- Product screenshots, recordings, customer material, testimonials, or logos.
- Comparisons that state unmeasured product behavior.

## Prohibited public context

Do not include credentials, private project context, non-public source
locations, customer material, private conversations, internal schedules,
unpublished qualification results, or security findings. Public Issues,
branches, commits, pull requests, reviews, workflow logs, artifacts, and
deployments follow the same boundary.

## Media and brand

Only first-party assets listed in
[`public/brand/provenance.json`](../public/brand/provenance.json) may be used
without an additional provenance review. Every image needs meaningful alt
text unless it is explicitly decorative. Media files must remain below eight
megabytes and must not contain scripts, external references, tracking pixels,
or hidden metadata.
