## JavaScript and build dependencies

The exact dependency graph and versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`.
The preliminary check requires every non-development dependency to declare a
reviewed license expression and rejects missing or unapproved expressions.
Package-level copyright and notice files remain available in each installed
package and its upstream distribution.
